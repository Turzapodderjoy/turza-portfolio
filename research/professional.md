# Research: Personal websites of non-designer professionals

**Scope:** personal/branded sites of business, operations, analytical, marketing and executive people — NOT visual designers, photographers or artists. Closest match to our subject (Denials Manager & Operations Analyst, healthcare RCM, Power BI / Retool / Python, Dhaka, targeting global recruiters, HR, founders, CEOs).

**Date:** October 2026 · **Method:** websearch listicles + direct fetch of source articles + 10 direct spot-checks of live sites.

**Sources used:** sitebuilderreport.com (22 consulting + 35 personal sites), nanoglobals.com (9 marketing consultants), jakestpeter.com (15 personal sites, homepages re-fetched Oct 10–13 2026), careerfoundry.com (analytics portfolios), hubspot.com, sitelyx.co, searchbloom.com (CEO brand sites), allianceinteractive.com, huntyourtribe.com, blog.theinterviewguys.com, digitalcvmaker.com (operations-manager site anatomy).

## Recurring design patterns for this professional (non-designer) category

1. **Résumé-led single page with anchor nav.** Hero (name + target role) → About → Experience → Projects/Work → Contact, all on one scroll with a sticky or in-page nav. Multi-page marketing architecture is rare at career level; it appears mainly for consultants selling services (Selina Johnson, Brian Honigman, Taylor Scher).

2. **Quantified proof replaces visual flair.** Stat counters (5+ yrs / 15+ dashboards), outcome bullets with deltas (12 days → 1, 58% deflection, +333% demos), and named case studies carry the page. Design is deliberately restrained — plain type, one accent colour, generous whitespace — so numbers are the loudest element.

3. **One primary next step, and it's recruiter-shaped for job-seekers.** Résumé PDF download + email/LinkedIn (Eisenstein, Yuvaraj, LaMonica, Brittany Chiang) versus a single booking link for independents (Taylor Scher "Book A Call", Selina Johnson, Daniel Miessler). Almost never two competing CTAs.

4. **Skill strip → tagged project cards → proof chain.** The tool stack (Power BI, DAX, SQL, Python, Excel, HIPAA) is surfaced as a chip/badge/bars row, and every project card repeats its stack tags plus a "Business Impact" line — a direct skills→evidence link a recruiter can scan in seconds.

5. **Niche named in the first sentence.** The strongest sites say who they help and in what domain before anything else ("Healthcare & E-commerce analytics", "SEO for B2B SaaS", "banking operations and 30-clinic health network"). Domain specificity (healthcare/RCM language) is the cheapest credibility signal in this category — relevant for denials/revenue-cycle positioning.

6. **One human detail, used deliberately.** A pet, a hobby page, a "currently" block or an "open to roles" badge (Lily Ray's DJ pages, Taylor Scher's dog, Eisenstein's open-to-work badge) — personality appears as a single controlled accent rather than as decoration.
